## 🎮 What is BetterCraft?

**BetterCraft** is a **game (game name)** built **on top of Mineclonia**, with the goal of going beyond the vanilla Minecraft experience by adding content, automation, technical improvements, and new gameplay systems.

While **Mineclonia** aims to stay faithful to the original Minecraft, **BetterCraft** focuses on:

- Content expansion  
- Automation and game logic  
- Greater variety of mobs, blocks, and structures  
- Advanced commands and systems  

---

## ✨ Main Differences Compared to Mineclonia

### 🔧 Systems and mechanics

- 🧱 **Dedicated Crafter Block**: automated crafting through a dedicated block  
- 🧠 **Smart and automatable crafting**, designed for integration with logic systems  
- 🎒 **Pouches**: additional portable storage  

### 🧱 World and blocks

- 🧱 **Moreblocks**: a wide variety of decorative and structural blocks  
- 🌳 **Pale Garden**: new biomes, natural variations, and environmental content  
- 🏰 **More structures**: new world-generated structures for exploration  

### 🧟 Mobs and entities

- 👁️ **Warden**: an advanced mob with unique behavior  
- 🐢 **Turtles**: new entities with their own mechanics  
- 🌲 **Creaking**: additional environmental entities  
- 🦴 **Skeleton variants**: multiple types with different behaviors  

### ⌨️ Commands and technical systems

- ⌨️ **Improved and more flexible commands**  
- 📍 **Reference coordinate system**:
  - `~~~` relative coordinates  
  - `^^^` directional coordinates  

These systems allow for more complex commands than those available in standard Mineclonia.
